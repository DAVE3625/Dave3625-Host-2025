## LLMs and prompt engineering

This lab is a slide deck — there is no notebook and no environment to set up.

**Start here: [Lab7.pdf](./Lab7.pdf)** (15 slides)

It covers what makes a good prompt — clarity, specificity, context, choice of language, persona, tone and style, splitting up complex requests, and iterating — followed by self-study exercises.

Nothing to install. Just read the slides and try the exercises against an LLM of your choice.
